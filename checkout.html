By subscribing you agree to the terms of sale, the refund policy and the privacy policy.

What happens after you pay

  1. Paddle emails you a receipt with a transaction ID.
  2. Open Bitcoffer → Settings → Licence and copy the machine fingerprint shown there.
  3. Email both — the fingerprint and the Paddle transaction ID — to ruohuyang@163.com, from the address you paid with.
  4. You get a device-bound activation serial for that Mac. Licences are issued by hand, so allow up to one business day.

Not in a hurry? The 30-day trial covers the direct download in full, and the Mac App Store version subscribes through Apple instead.
