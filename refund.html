Bitcoffer Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15 · Applies to direct-download licences bought through Paddle

In one sentence: ask within 14 days of being charged and you get your money back, no questions asked. The licence that went with it is cancelled.

1 · Direct-download licences

  • First purchase — full refund if you ask within 14 days of the charge. You do not need a reason.
  • Renewals — the same 14 days applies to each annual renewal charge, counted from the renewal date.
  • After 14 days — no refund for the remaining part of the year, but you can stop the next renewal at any time; the licence simply runs out at the end of the paid year.

2 · How to ask

Email ruohuyang@163.com from the address you paid with, and include the Paddle transaction ID from your receipt. Paddle processes the refund to the original payment method; how quickly it appears depends on your bank, typically 5–10 business days.

3 · What happens to the licence

A refunded licence is cancelled, so paste-in serials stop activating new devices. Export your vault before you ask if you want to keep the data — refunds never touch your local vault, which is encrypted on your Mac and never uploaded.

4 · Card disputes

If something looks wrong with a charge, email first. A chargeback freezes the licence while the bank investigates, which is slower for everyone than a refund.

5 · Mac App Store version

Purchases and subscriptions made through the Mac App Store are billed and refunded by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com for those.

6 · Your statutory rights

Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer law of your own country, including the EU/UK 14-day right of withdrawal for digital purchases.
