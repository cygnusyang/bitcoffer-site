US$9.99／年，按年续费

这是直装版（非 App Store 版）的年度授权。付款由 Paddle 处理，它是本交易的卖方：销售税／增值税由 Paddle 计算并申报，付款前会显示含税总价。

付款即表示同意销售条款、退款政策与隐私政策。

付款之后

  1. Paddle 会发一封带交易编号的收据到你填写的邮箱。
  2. 打开 Bitcoffer →「设置」→「许可」，复制页面上显示的本机指纹。
  3. 用付款时填写的邮箱，把指纹和 Paddle 交易编号一起发到 ruohuyang@163.com。
  4. 你会收到绑定这台 Mac 的激活序列号。序列号是人工签发的，通常一个工作日内发出。

不着急的话，可以先用 30 天免费试用；Mac App Store 版则走 Apple 的内购订阅。

This page sells the annual direct-download licence (US$9.99/year, two Macs). Payment is handled by Paddle, the seller of record; taxes are calculated and remitted by Paddle and the final total is shown before you pay.

After paying, copy the machine fingerprint from Bitcoffer → Settings → Licence and email it with your Paddle transaction ID to ruohuyang@163.com. Serials are issued by hand, usually within one business day. The English checkout page has the same steps.
