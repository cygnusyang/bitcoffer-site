Get your Bitcoffer licence

Paste the machine fingerprint from Bitcoffer and your device-bound serial appears right away.

In Bitcoffer open Settings → Licence → Machine fingerprint and press Copy. The serial is emailed to the address you paid with as well.


  

  

    
  

  
Open Bitcoffer → Settings → Licence, paste the serial, press Activate. Keep this page or the email: the same link re-issues this serial if you reinstall macOS, and issues one for a second Mac.
